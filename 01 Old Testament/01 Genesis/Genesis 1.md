| --------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| vv. 9-25  | Creation of All Things       | God created day and night, sun and star, land, seas, animals and creatures |
| vv. 26-31 | Creation of Man              | God created man in his image                                               |

### Flow of the Chapter

1. **vv. 1-2**
   - The beginning of the creation

1. **vv. 3-13**
   - God creates large realms like light/darkness, seas/land/sky 

1. **vv. 14-25**
   - God creates vegetation/animals for the seas/land/sky
   - God creates the sun and stars/moon
	   - The greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night

1. **vv. 26-31**
   - God created man in his image and gave man dominion over the earth
