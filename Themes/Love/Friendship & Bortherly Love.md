# Theme
> Modern Category: Platonic Love
## Definition
- Loving someone without romantic or sexual intention
## Distinctions/Boundaries
- This theme includes two closely related expressions of non-romantic love:
	**Friendship**
	- Voluntary relational bonds
	- Loyalty, companionship, honesty, encouragement, sacrifice
	**Brotherly/Familial Love**
	- Family-like affection among believers
	- Honor, service, care, purity, mutual responsibility

- This theme does not include:
	- Romantic attraction 
	- Sexual desire
	- Marital intimacy 
	See [[Romantic & Marital Love]]

## Key Passages
### [[John 15#John 15:12-13]]
	"This is my commandment, that you love one another
	as I have loved you. Greater love has no one than
	this, that someone lay down his life for his
	friends."

**Observations**
- Jesus commands his disciples to love one another
- The standard is the way Jesus has loved them
- The greatest expression describe is giving one's life for friends

**Interpretation**
- Jesus is speaking to his disciple during his final teaching before his death. His own self-giving love becomes the model for how his disciples are to love one another.

**Contribution to Theme**
- Friendship and brotherly love are not merely feelings. Biblical love is willing to sacrifice for another person's good.

**Connections**
[[Sacrifice]] : [[Friendship]] : [[Jesus]] : [[The Cross]]
### [[Proverbs 17#Proverbs 17:17]]
	"A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born
	for adversity."

**Observations**
- A true friend continues to love "at all times"
- Adversity reveals the strength of close relationships

**Interpretation**
- Wisdom literature presents genuine friendship as dependable rather than conditional upon circumstances

**Contribution to Theme**
- Loyalty and constancy, especially during suffering, are marks of genuine friendship

**Connections**
[[Friendship]] : [[Loyalty]] : [[Suffering]] : [[Faithfulness]]
### [[Romans 12#Romans 12:10]]
	"Love one another ith bortherly affection. Outdo
	one another in showing honor."

**Observations**
- Believers are commanded to have brotherly affection
- They are also commanded to honor one another

**Interpretation**
- Describes the transformed life of God's people. Paul portrays the church not merely as an organization but as a family whose members show genuine affection and honor toward one another.

**Contribution to Theme**
- Christian brotherly love is family-like. It involves affection, honor, humility, and putting others before oneself.

### [[1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4:9]]
	"Now concerning brotherly love you have no need for
	anyone to write to you, for you yourselves have
	been taught by God to love one another,"

**Observations**
- Paul says the Thessalonians have been taught by God to love one another 
- Brotherly love is expected among Christians

**Interpretation**
- Brotherly love is part of the sanctified life Paul has been describing. It is something God produces in his people, yet believers are still called to grow in it.

**Contribution to Theme**
- Brotherly love is not optional in Christian community. It should continually grow and become more evident. 

**Connections**
[[Sanctification]] : [[Church]] : [[Christian Community]] : [[Love]]
### [[1 Timothy 5#1 Timothy 5:1-2]]
	"Do not rebuke an older man but encourage him as
	 you would a father, younger men as brother, older
	  women as mothers, younger women as sisters, in
	  all purity."

**Observations**
- Timothy is told to treat older believes as fathers and mothers 
- Younger believers are to be treated as brothers and sisters
- Paul explicitly adds "in all purity" regarding younger women

**Interpretation**
- Paul gives Timothy a family model for relationships within the church. Relationships between men and women do not have to become romantic; they can be characterized by familial affection, respect, and purity.

**Contribution to Theme**
- This passage gives an especially useful biblical framework for non-romantic relationships: treat fellow believers as family, with respect, affection, appropriate boundaries, and purity.
